index.html currently references the logo as:

  ns-logo-no-bg.png   - main header/footer logo
  favicon.png         - small square icon for the browser tab (still needed)

If you replace the logo with a differently-named file, update the
src="assets/logo/..." references in index.html to match (there are two: the
header <a class="brand"> and the footer <div class="footer-brand">).

If the logo file is missing or fails to load, the site automatically falls
back to showing the text "Namo Siddhivinayak" in its place.
