About Namo Siddhivinayak

We're Campsie's dedicated grocer for the subcontinent and the Pacific — and the oldest Indian grocery store in the area, family-run and serving the community for over 20 years. We bring Indian, Nepali and Fiji Islander groceries together under one roof, with trusted brands at genuinely reasonable prices.

Campsie itself makes for an easy trip: it's close to the city and well served by train and bus, and the area is also known for cheap, convenient fresh fruit and vegetables — so a visit to us can easily be a one-stop shop. We're an easy trip for shoppers across Campsie, Canterbury, Belmore, Lakemba, Earlwood, Ashfield, Croydon Park, Belfield and the surrounding Sydney suburbs.
